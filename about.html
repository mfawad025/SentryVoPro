About Us

Built by someone who did this work by hand, for years.

SentryVo started as a Fiverr gig. Since 2020, I've handled DMCA takedowns and copyright enforcement directly for content creators — manually searching for leaks, tracking down the right contact for each site, and filing notice after notice by hand. The work was effective, but it didn't scale: every new client meant repeating the same searches, the same lookups, the same emails, one at a time. SentryVo is that same process, automated — built specifically because I was already doing it, for real clients, and knew exactly where the manual version broke down.

Our Mission

We believe creators should own their content and their income — not have it siphoned off by anonymous pirates. Everything we build serves that one goal.

Our Approach

Instead of a manual, ticket-based takedown process, we automate detection and filing so cases move faster than doing it by hand.

Who's Behind It

SentryVo is built and run by one person, not a faceless support queue — the same person who's been doing this work by hand for creators since 2020. When you reach out, you're talking to the person who actually understands your case.
