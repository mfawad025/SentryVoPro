Add a client's original photos

Upload the client's own original photos (and screenshots/frames from their videos). SentryVo turns each one into a short fingerprint and discards the image — only the fingerprint is stored. The scanner then recognises re-uploads of those photos, even when resized, compressed, watermarked or flipped, and marks them [Verified match]. More distinct photos means better coverage (up to 300 per client). This is not face recognition.
