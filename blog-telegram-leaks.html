What to Do When Your Content Is Leaked on Telegram

Telegram works differently from most websites. This guide explains what you can report, where to send it, and what to expect.

Why Telegram leaks are different

Telegram content is shared in channels, groups and bots, and many of them are small, private or get renamed. A web search will only show you a fraction of what is there. Taking one channel down often does not stop the content from reappearing in another, so reporting needs to be repeated and tracked.

What Telegram will and will not act on

According to Telegram's own FAQ, copyright holders should send takedown requests by email to dmca@telegram.org. Telegram says this covers public content such as channels, bots, sticker sets and public groups. It says it does not process requests about private groups and private chats. Requests should come from the copyright owner or someone authorized to act for them.

In practice that means content in a public channel can be reported, but content shared only in a private group cannot be removed through this route.

What to include in your email

  • Your name and contact details, and the name of the copyright owner if you are acting for them.
  • The exact Telegram links to the content, in the form t.me/channelname/123. A link to the channel alone is not enough if you are reporting specific posts.
  • A description of your original work and where the original is published.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the owner.
  • Your signature.

How to collect the links

  1. Search Google for your name together with site:t.me to find public posts that Google has indexed.
  2. Open each link and confirm it shows your content. Take a screenshot with the date visible for your own records.
  3. Copy the full link of each post. Do not retype them.
  4. Group links by channel in your email so they are easy to review.

After you report

Keep a copy of every email and the date you sent it. Check the links again after a few days. If a channel is removed but a new one shows the same content, report the new one too. Do not join leak channels or message their owners: it puts your accounts and your safety at risk and rarely helps.

Be careful what you claim

Only report content you own or are authorized to act for. Reporting other people's content as yours, or reporting posts that do not contain your work, is not allowed and can have legal consequences.
