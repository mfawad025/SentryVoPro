FAQ

Before you get protected

How fast are leaks removed?

Most confirmed leaks trigger an automated DMCA notice within hours of detection. More complex cases, like disputed ownership or unresponsive hosts, are escalated and followed up manually.

Which platforms do you cover?

Scanning covers major creator platforms, social media, search engines, forums and messaging apps, regardless of where your original content is hosted.

What if I'm a faceless creator?

No problem. Detection is based on your stage name, aliases and the specific platforms you're on, not facial recognition. Your face doesn't need to appear in the content for us to find a leak.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There's no long-term contract. Cancel from your dashboard whenever you like.

Do you store my photos?

No. If you send us original photos for fingerprint matching, each one is converted into a short fingerprint and the photo itself is discarded. The fingerprint can't be turned back into the image.

Is this facial recognition?

No. Fingerprint matching compares the photo itself, not faces, so it only recognizes copies of the specific photos you provide, including resized, compressed, watermarked or flipped versions. It doesn't identify people and uses no biometric data.

Will my identity stay private?

Your personal information is never included in the public-facing DMCA notice, and account data is kept confidential and encrypted.

What is a DMCA takedown service?

It's a service that files copyright removal requests on your behalf under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, so you don't have to track down and report every instance of stolen content yourself.
