Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

14-day refund on your first payment

If SentryVo isn't right for you, email support@sentryvo.com within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, and refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Renewals and cancellation

You can cancel at any time from your account or by email. Cancellation stops future billing and your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Renewal payments are not refunded, but if you were charged by mistake or did not use the Service after a renewal, write to us within 7 days and we will review it.

What we can't refund

Takedown results depend on third-party hosts and search engines, so we can't promise removal of any specific link. A host not acting on a notice is not a reason for a refund after the 14-day window, but tell us and we will re-report the link and escalate it.

How to ask

Email support@sentryvo.com from the address on your account. We reply within 2 business days.
